About

A research profile connecting physical geography, geospatial technologies and environmental change.

My work investigates the coupled processes that shape river basins—from tectonic and litho-structural controls to climate variability and anthropogenic pressures.

My doctoral research focuses on the hydrogeomorphic evolution of the Brahmani River Basin in eastern India, with particular interests in landscape evolution, erosion, sediment connectivity and sustainable watershed management.

Research

Key themes across my doctoral research and broader scientific interests.

01 / RIVER BASINS

Hydro-geomorphic evolution

River longitudinal profiles, landscape evolution and the influence of tectonic and litho-structural controls on channel form.

02 / SEDIMENT

Erosion & connectivity

Soil erosion, sediment dynamics, sediment delivery and connectivity assessment using process-based and geospatial approaches.

03 / CLIMATE

Hydro-climatic variability

Understanding how rainfall variability and changing environmental conditions influence watershed processes and hazards.

04 / HUMAN IMPACT

Anthropogenic controls

Assessing how land-use change and human interventions modify geomorphic processes and sediment regimes.

05 / GEOINFORMATICS

Remote sensing & GIS

Integrating satellite data, DEMs, GIS, Google Earth Engine and spatial modelling for river-basin research.

06 / APPLICATION

Watershed management

Translating geomorphic and hydrological understanding into evidence for sustainable watershed planning and hazard assessment.
